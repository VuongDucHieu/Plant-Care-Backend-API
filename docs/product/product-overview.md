# Plant Care Platform

## Product Vision

Plant Care Platform helps users identify plants, understand plant health, diagnose possible diseases, and receive actionable care recommendations.

The platform combines plant-management capabilities with AI-assisted plant analysis.

## Target Users 

### Plant Owners

Users who own plants and need help identifying, monitoring, or caring for them.

### Beginner Gardeners

Users who do not have enough botanical knowledge to identify plant species or recognize plant-health problems.

### Plant Enthusiasts

Users who want to learn more about plants and maintain a personal plant collection.

## Core Problems

Users may have difficulty:

- Identifying an unknown plant
- Understanding plant requirements
- Recognizing plant diseases
- Determining appropriate treatment
- Remembering recurring plant-care activities

## Core Capabilities

The platform is expected to evolve around the following capabilities:

1. Plant Identification
2. Plant Diagnosis
3. Treatment Recommendations
4. Plant Collection
5. Care Scheduling
6. Care Reminders
7. Diagnosis History

## Product Principle

AI Providers are implementation details.

Product requirements describe user-facing capabilities and expected system behavior rather than depending on a specific AI provider.
